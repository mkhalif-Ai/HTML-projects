about us

MKLI Digital School is not simply about covering the syllabus.
It focuses heavily on exam mastery—understanding what KCSE tests,
recognizing common question patterns, learning how marks are awarded,
and developing the ability to construct answers that earn those marks.
It combines teacher experience, structured revision, performance analysis and
technology/AI-assisted approaches to make preparation more targeted.
